### Adapted Components & Architectural Patterns:
- **Bank / Inventory UI:** Grid-based item tile layout, selection panel, stack display, item detail modal, and bulk sell/equip action patterns.
- **Equipment Sheet UI:** Character slot layout (weapon, helmet, platebody, platelegs, shield, boots, ring/amulet) with equipped item popovers.
- **Town / Settlement Presentation:** Building cards with tier progress bars, passive production displays, and multi-cost upgrade cards.
- **Combat Presentation Patterns:** Clean double-panel combat HUD (player vitals & attack bar vs. enemy vitals & attack bar).
- **CSS Design Tokens & Components:** Dark-themed idle RPG tokens, cards, progress bars, and tab panels.
