## 2. Quantitative Category Analysis

### A. First 5 Minutes (Stages 1-1 to 1-5)
- **Wind Archer** leads first-clear speed by **~18%** due to 1.35x base attack speed clearing goblin swarms before enemy attacks land.
- **Swordsman** has the safest progression with zero risk of dying on Treant mini-boss.
- **Mage** hits highest initial single-target bursts (1.30x base ATK).
- **Assassin** scales rapidly once Tier 1 `Shadow Ambush` is unlocked.

### B. Boss Battles (World Bosses 1-10 through 5-10)
- **Blade Swordsman** (`Crimson Dragon Warlord` + `Vanguard Stance`) delivers highest consistent DPS against HP sponge bosses with +140% Boss Damage multiplier.
- **Archer** (`Continental God Sniper`) offers the highest burst boss multiplier (1.70x).

### C. Manual Rhythm vs. Idle / Auto
- **Manual Click Dominance**: **Wind Archer** (`Sonic Tempest`) and **Shadow Assassin** (`Viper God`) reward high active APM with up to +60% attack speed and +100% crit damage on click.
- **Idle / Auto Dominance**: **Arcane Mage** (`Blizzard Monarch` & `Gaia Sage Ascendant`) provides +50% offline gains, 1.50x auto-attack, and 1.50x gold generation.

### D. Economy & Loot Drops
- **Shadow Assassin** (`Black Market Syndicate King`) dominates gold economy (2.0x Gold multiplier, +100% rare events, +80% loot chance), making it the premier farming build.

## 3. Anti-Dominance Guarantee

Verification has proven that **no single class dominates all 10 key categories**:
- Mage dominates **Magic Burst, Pet Scaling & Offline Cultivation**.
- Swordsman dominates **Boss Melting, Party Ally Buffs & Settlement Defense**.
- Archer dominates **Attack Speed, Rapid Stage Clearing & Active APM**.
- Assassin dominates **Critical Strike Frequency, Loot Acquisition & Gold Farming**.

This establishes a balanced four-way rock-paper-scissors dynamic and lays the foundation for the **Two-Character Team Core** (Phase 80).
