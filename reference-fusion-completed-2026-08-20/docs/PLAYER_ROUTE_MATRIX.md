### 2. Detailed Archetype Specifications

#### Route 1: The Lawful Mountain Protector (Swordsman)
- **Combat Class**: Blade Swordsman (Heavy Slash & Vanguard Armor).
- **Companion Pet**: Aegis Golem (Earth element, $+25\%$ DEF & Damage Absorption).
- **Key Equipment**: Vanguard Greatsword (Forged by Master Goran, $+35\%$ Boss DMG, $+25$ DEF).
- **Mercenary Ally**: Boran Ironshield ($+15\%$ Boss DMG, $+30$ Settlement DEF).
- **Moral Alignment**: Virtuous Karma ($+65$).
- **Story Decisions**:
  - Rescues wounded royal knight in *The Lost Heir of Eldoria*.
  - Exposes usurper to the sovereign court.
  - Builds Mountain Haven into a flourishing Citadel.
  - Repels all goblin and cultist raids with high defense.
- **Earned Titles**: *Virtuous Champion*, *Pioneer Lord*, *Hero of the Realm*.
- **Legacy Ending**: **Savior of the Mountain Realm** ($+25\%$ Power Multiplier permanent Samsara buff).

---

#### Route 2: The Dark Sovereign of Shadow (Assassin)
- **Combat Class**: Shadow Assassin (High Crit Rate, Critical Surge, Flurry Strike).
- **Companion Pet**: Fenrir Shadowhound (Dark/Ice element, $+25\%$ Crit Damage).
- **Key Equipment**: Cursed Bloodblade (Purchased from Smuggler Malik, $+45\%$ Crit DMG, $-15\%$ DEF).
- **Mercenary Ally**: Sylas the Whisper ($+22\%$ Crit DMG, $+8\%$ Attack Speed).
- **Moral Alignment**: Infamous Karma ($-75$).
- **Story Decisions**:
  - Robs dying knight and sells royal seal to the underground network.
  - Recruits Vane the Shadowsmith to forge void daggers.
  - Embraces forbidden Black Market artifacts and Blood Oaths.
  - Rules Mountain Haven through terror and intimidation.
- **Earned Titles**: *Dread Overlord*, *Shadow Kingpin*.
- **Legacy Ending**: **Dread Sovereign of the Void** ($+40\%$ Crit Damage permanent Samsara buff).

---

#### Route 3: The Unbound Wanderer (Archer / Ranger)
- **Combat Class**: Valley Archer (Rapid Fire, Piercing Arrow, High Attack Speed).
- **Companion Pet**: Sylph Sprite (Wind element, $+20\%$ Attack Speed & Loot Multiplier).
- **Key Equipment**: Windforce Composite Bow (Swift draw, $+30\%$ Manual Speed).
- **Mercenary Ally**: Zephyr Windstrider ($+22\%$ Attack Speed, $+12\%$ Click Impact).
- **Moral Alignment**: Pure Neutral Karma ($0$).
- **Story Decisions**:
  - Avoids political entanglements between the crown and shadow syndicates.
  - Focuses on deep wilderness exploration, tracking the Ancient Thunder Drake.
  - Maintains trade caravans without factional loyalty.
  - Wanders freely between mountain passes.
- **Earned Titles**: *The Eternal Wanderer*, *Master Explorer*, *Rhythm Master*.
- **Legacy Ending**: **The Eternal Wanderer** ($+20\%$ Attack Speed permanent Samsara buff).

---

#### Route 4: The Celestial Ascendant (Mage)
- **Combat Class**: Arcane Mage (Elemental Burst, Arcane Shield, Spell Damage).
- **Companion Pet**: Ignis Drake (Fire element, $+25\%$ Spell Power).
- **Key Equipment**: Celestial Starlight Staff (Forged from Meteorite Star Ore).
- **Mercenary Ally**: Kaelen Sunspark ($+18\%$ Spell Attack, $+8\%$ Power Multiplier).
- **Moral Alignment**: Enlightened Ascendant ($+30$).
- **Story Decisions**:
  - Solves the glyphs of the Ancient Celestial Vault in *The Sunken Star Ore*.
  - Conquers Tower Floor 50+.
  - Ascends through multiple Samsara rebirth cycles.
- **Earned Titles**: *Tower Ascendant*, *Cosmic Sovereign*, *Grand Architect*.
- **Legacy Ending**: **Celestial Ascendant** ($+35\%$ Boss Damage permanent Samsara buff).
